NEWS SOURCE/DATE COMPLETION — PROSPECTIVE VERSION 1
4 October 2026. No News confirmatory results exist.

The narrative prescribed a source-disjoint 2017 calibration population and a
2018–2019 analysis population but did not fully serialize their joint allocation.
news_calendar.py now makes that choice explicit before semantic results:

1. Keep the existing source hash, serialization, salt and exact 70/15/15 partition.
2. Calibration uses only calibration-assigned domains' 2017 records.
3. Analysis uses only train-assigned domains' 2018–2019 records.
4. Test-domain records are reserved, unused in this structural branch. All other
   domain/year combinations receive explicit cohort-exclusion ledger entries.
5. Unknown-source records remain separately named singletons. Domain inference
   retains the adapter's pinned PSL/URL consistency rules and qualifications.
6. Whole-source size panels are built separately within the guarded analysis
   period. Calendar-window membership means complete eligible records of chosen
   months, not all-time source completeness or complete web/publisher holdings.

prepare_news_cohorts(adapter_directory, coverage_path, output_dir) returns a new
sealed ccu-news-preparation-1 audit, not fabricated phase3 provenance. It streams
calibration.jsonl and analysis.jsonl, then writes records.jsonl after removing
train-side normalized exact copies of calibration. Exact comparison uses a disk
SQLite text index with a 2 MiB page cache. No News parent/native-duplicate link
relation is invented. Source-ID sets are metadata stored in memory. Raw cohort
outputs remain local, with all their disk bytes charged; never redistribute the
News bodies in the project backup. The full-row and text/ID SHA bindings are
computed as streaming canonical JSON arrays, identical to phase3 digest semantics.

The prepared audit fields are schema, stage=fixed_guards_done_semantic_guard_pending,
prepared_records_file_sha256, prepared_rows_sha256, normalized_records_sha256,
news_preparation_code_sha256, adapter_code_sha256, source_and_scoring_code_sha256,
and the explicit allocation/coverage bindings. The encoding/calibration bridge
must recognize this separate schema and preserve its partition assignments.
Do not reapply the generic random split to replace the date/source cohort rules.

CALENDAR COVERAGE MANIFEST
schema: ccu-news-calendar-coverage-1
input_file_sha256: exact original extracted News CSV/JSONL bytes
completeness_unit: all_records_in_pinned_extraction_for_recorded_month
evidence: nonempty list of {path,sha256} local acquisition/coverage evidence files
months: every month 2017-01 through 2019-12 mapped to
        {status: complete|incomplete|unavailable, basis: nonempty explanation}

No completeness is inferred from a count, an earliest/latest observed date, or
the mere presence of a few rows. SHA bindings establish consistency only; a
human must verify what the extraction/acquisition evidence actually establishes.
The module reports whether all 2017 months were declared complete and preserves
every declared status. Missing/changed evidence is refused. There is no bundled
manifest falsely marking the existing connector preview as complete.

FINAL CALENDAR RULE
Compute month counts only after the fixed and primary E5 semantic guards.
Select the earliest declared-complete calendar month from January 2018 onward
whose eligible count reaches 10,000, including the whole month. If no individual
complete month qualifies, take a consecutive complete whole-month prefix starting
January 2018, including the boundary month in full. Stop at the first coverage
gap; never join across it while claiming a complete prefix. Report shortfall,
overshoot, every month count/status and a stopped-at-gap field. With insufficient
coverage or records the arm remains unavailable/under target; do not substitute
a favorable later corpus or silently lower the target.
