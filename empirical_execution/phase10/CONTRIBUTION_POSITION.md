# Paper position after the finite-format qualification

The strongest paper remains about repair after deletion changes semantic curation.
Its target is `A(C(D minus F))`.
The work must connect that target to natural admissions and their learning consequences.
The new theorem strengthens the memory account under a precise implementation model.
It does not replace the missing empirical evidence.

## Proposed central claim

Deleting raw records can expose retained examples that never trained the original learner.
The remaining deletion horizon determines which excluded payloads may still matter.
This can require substantial private state even when all currently observable heads are identical.
Our construction realizes that obstruction with shared stored features, fixed binary labels, and public forgotten payloads.
It supplies an exact sequential codec on the same family.

The broad prediction-versus-counterfactual distinction is established elsewhere.
The paper should claim the specified curation-to-learning construction and its qualified repair results.
Do not claim a new information-theoretic principle.

The empirical question remains whether the mathematical obstruction matters in realistic NLP pipelines.

## Scope that must remain visible

The theorem uses a fixed public universe and cumulative record-deletion budget.
It fixes representations, pair eligibility, priorities, and threshold.
It does not cover arbitrary corpus-fitted clustering or representation refitting.
Its canonical state means logical byte equality under the declared contract.
It does not prove physical erasure or transcript privacy.
Its finite-output bridge requires a real certificate against the exact target.
A configured tolerance alone is insufficient.
