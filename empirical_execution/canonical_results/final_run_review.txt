Target guarantee
The certificate checks exact PSD of the retained aggregate Gram core. Hence
G=U A U^T is PSD, and for n>0, G+lambda*n I >= lambda*n I > 0. This establishes
positive definiteness of the ridge Hessian, not necessarily of Gram itself.
Each fresh comparison rebuilds from retained rows and a rescored retained
curator graph; byte equality binds the repaired target to that exact rebuild.
The independent rational algebra tests support the canonicalizer and certificate
arithmetic. This is a guarantee for exact aggregation of the frozen binary
inputs, not identical floating summation to the legacy training implementation.
