BLINDED PAIR JUDGMENT GUIDE — PREPARED, NOT YET COLLECTED
======================================================

Judge the text pair independently before seeing any other rater's answer. You must
not see similarity scores, source/method names, deletion requests, original task
labels, downstream model behavior, previous judgments, or the desired outcome.
The study coordinator assigns each pair to three distinct independent people.
Each person completes only their own assigned copy. A repeated pair in the later
validation stage has a fresh blinded assignment; earlier answers must not be copied.

Choose exactly one category:

exact_copy
    The same text or a purely superficial formatting/spelling variation, without
    a change in assertion, entity, quantity, negation, time, or information.

substantially_same_meaning
    The texts express substantially the same task-relevant meaning. Different
    wording is allowed, but neither text adds or changes a consequential assertion.

overlapping_information
    They share some information, but at least one contains a meaningful addition,
    omission, conflict, or distinction. Shared topic alone does not establish sameness.

merely_related
    They concern related topics or situations but make different assertions.

unrelated
    They do not share meaningful content for this judgment.

uncertain
    You cannot decide reliably. Do not guess. Use this for insufficient context,
    ambiguous quotation/irony, language you cannot assess, or unclear distinctions.

Pay particular attention to changed entities, numbers, negation, dates/times and
claims. A consequential change prevents the positive categories even when most
words are shared. Annotators receive the fixed corpus/task definition without gold
labels; no additional classification labels are invented for news.

The recorded binary compatibility field is mechanically determined:
    exact_copy or substantially_same_meaning -> duplicate
    overlapping_information, merely_related or unrelated -> not_duplicate
    uncertain -> unsure
Retain the original six-category response. The analysis treats unsure conservatively
as nonpositive and counts a pair positive only if at least2 of3 raters are positive.

The coordinator must preserve independent responses before any adjudication.
Adjudicated answers do not replace primary independent ratings. The prepared blank
template does not establish that collection, informed participation, content handling
or compensation has occurred; these arrangements remain required before real use.
