PROSPECTIVE PREPARATION AMENDMENT - 4 OCTOBER 2026

Authority: the original empirical protocol narrative remains authoritative where
the compact study_design.json was ambiguous. No confirmatory data or human ratings
have been collected. These definitions are locked before the primary study;
they were not previously specified at this implementation detail.

1. Hash serialization is UTF8(salt + NUL + source_unit_id), unsigned SHA256 with
   exact integer quantile tests. Source groups remain whole. Priority uses the
   narrative SHA256(priority-v1|seed|record-id), seed0 primary; ties use record ID.

2. Exact text and native duplicate-link precedence is test > calibration > train.
   Remove lower-precedence endpoints against the original assigned population.
   Civil parent filtering only removes a training CHILD whose observed parent
   is calibration/test. Other cross-partition parent directions are logged, not
   silently removed. Missing parents do not establish independence; unresolved
   links and snapshot completeness require review. Sentinels null, empty,0,-1
   (including string forms) mean no parent. Community Stack owners<=0 become
   explicit unknown singletons, never authentic author-withdrawal sources.

3. Normalized record text is NFC plus whitespace collapse. Preserve original_fields
   unchanged. Versioned intake compares normalized original and prepared text;
   it no longer demands raw-byte equality after a prescribed normalization.

4. One shared finite-precision scorer is authoritative for calibration, semantic
   leakage filtering and fixed-graph reconstruction. For each frozen FP32 vector,
   promote to FP64, sum coordinate squares in order, divide by the computed square
   root, and sum pair products in coordinate order. Use strict score>tau. Do not
   clip scores; only score-bin indices saturate at the endpoints. This definition
   removes batching/context dependence of the computational target within the
   locked runtime. Historical phase2 BLAS-based engineering outputs are untouched.

   Locality lemma. For fixed ID-derived priorities, frozen vectors and threshold,
   the directed graph after deleting F equals the induced graph G(D)[D\F].
   Proof: each surviving edge is decided by the same two frozen vectors, the same
   sequence of floating operations, the same threshold and ID priorities. No
   other row enters the predicate. Therefore every surviving pair has the same
   edge indicator. This proves computational graph consistency, not semantic
   quality, graph sparsity, or a real-arithmetic cosine certificate.

5. Threshold selection preserves all six protocol categories. Only exact_copy
   and substantially_same_meaning are positive. A pair is positive with>=2 of3
   independent human positives; uncertain is conservative nonpositive. Selection
   uses a ratio of Horvitz-Thompson weighted counts within the sampled score bins.
   The first grid threshold with ratio>=.95 and nonzero support qualifies for
   development. Raw/effective support is reported, never mistaken for proof.

6. Independent validation uses SRS up to200 from ALL above-threshold calibration
   pairs; fresh assignments are made even on overlapping selection pairs. Invert
   the exact hypergeometric upper tail with rejection at tail<=.05. The lower
   count L is the least K for which P_K(X>=x)>.05; pass only when10L>=9N and the
   development selection qualified. Census is exact; an empty population has no
   defined precision. No retuning after validation. Conditional coverage concerns
   fixed/potential outcomes under the stated majority protocol, not latent truth
   or additional annotator variation. Rater assignment must be independent of
   pair sampling, and authentic blinded collection must be independently verified.

7. Prepared-file, full-row, source-partition, cache-byte and calibration-frame
   bindings prevent accidental reuse with changed inputs. Seals establish
   consistency, not signatures or proof of human/corpus/model authenticity.
   Quality output always leaves confirmatory_study_ready=false. Passing the
   mechanical statistical gate cannot authorize a publication claim by itself.

8. Versioned intake_v2 corrects the earlier phase2 validator without overwriting
   historical code/results. Optional six-category annotations must match their
   binary collapse. A completed annotation template remains a claim by its
   collector; software cannot authenticate its human origin.

Remaining actual execution gates: original source-rich corpora, pinned E5 cache
or usable local encoder/tokenizer/runtime, genuine human selection and validation
ratings, provenance review, required panels, and the remaining pre-execution lock
(labels/vocabularies, lambdas, requests, resource policies and code/runtime hashes).
