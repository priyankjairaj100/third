\section{Feature-scale memory from realizable counterfactual ridge heads}
\label{sec:realizable-feature-memory}

The arbitrary-statistic query-rank lower bound does not apply directly to
the constrained positive-semidefinite, rank-one contributions of ridge
regression.  The binary-label activation-frontier construction establishes
one hidden bit per candidate, but does not account for feature dimension.
We close this particular gap with a family of actual ridge problems whose
same unit features determine curation and prediction.  This is a worst-case
model-query state theorem, not a total-byte optimality or empirical claim.

\subsection{Access and learner contract}
For $S$ with $n>0$, the prescribed average-loss learner minimizes
\[
 L_S(W)=\frac1{2n}\sum_{v\in S}\|z_v^TW-y_v^T\|_2^2
                      +\frac\lambda2\|W\|_F^2,
 \qquad W\in\mathbb R^{d\times q},\quad\lambda>0.
\]
For $n=0$ the target is zero.  Curation is earlier-raw-neighbor suppression
at strict cosine threshold $2/5$, with fixed order.  Queries are cumulative
identifier sets of size at most the stated horizon.  Public information
includes the fixed graph, identifiers, construction parameters, candidate
cap centers and frames, and fixed zero-label blockers.  The actual candidate
features and responses are private.  Every candidate-dependent deployment
head, cache, feature table, remote store, ticket, and retained private random
seed counts as state.  There is no retained-candidate reread or uncharged
data-dependent side channel.  Full forgotten payloads may accompany a
request; all probes below delete only fixed public records.

The exact-coordinate theorem assumes a deterministic encoder into a fixed
$\mathbb R^s$, continuous on the specified open parameter domain.  No
decoder continuity is required.  Its upper bound is a model-query summary,
not a canonical deletion-clean state implementation.  The separate bit
theorem permits arbitrary encoders and randomization, with a state alphabet
of size at most $2^B$.

\subsection{A rational fixed-margin construction}
Fix $b\ge1$, $m\ge2$, $q\ge1$, and an ambient dimension $d$ admitting unit
signatures $u_1,\ldots,u_m\in\mathbb R^{d-2}$ such that
$|u_i^Tu_j|\le1/6$ for $i\ne j$.  One sufficient condition is
\[
 d-2\ge\left\lceil72\log(2m(m-1))\right\rceil:
\]
normalized independent sign vectors have pairwise failure probability at
most $2e^{-(d-2)/72}$, and a union bound gives positive success probability.
Alternatively orthogonal signatures suffice when $d\ge m+2$; zero padding
allows larger dimensions.  Arbitrarily large $m$ at fixed $d$ is not claimed.

In priority order put an anchor $a$, common blockers
$S_0=\{s_1,\ldots,s_b\}$, private blockers $p_1,\ldots,p_m$, and candidates
$w_1,\ldots,w_m$.  Let $e_0,e_1$ be orthogonal to the signature subspace and set
\[
 a=e_0,\qquad
 s_j=\tfrac8{17}e_0+\tfrac{15}{17}e_1,\qquad
 p_i=\tfrac8{17}e_0+\tfrac{15}{17}u_i,\qquad
 w_i^0=\tfrac8{17}e_1+\tfrac{15}{17}u_i.
\]
All have norm one.  At these centers, anchor--blocker similarity is $8/17$,
common--candidate similarity is $120/289$, and matching private--candidate
similarity is $225/289$.  Nonmatching private--candidate similarity is at
most $75/578$; distinct candidate or private-blocker similarity is at most
$203/578$; common--private similarity is $64/289$; anchor--candidate
similarity is zero.  The common blockers form a harmless clique.

Let $r=1/100$.  Vary each $w_i$ independently on the unit-sphere cap
$\|w_i-w_i^0\|_2<r$, while keeping every blocker fixed.  A single varying
endpoint changes an inner product by less than $r$, and two varying
endpoints change it by less than $2r$.  The tight required margins satisfy
\[
 \frac{120}{289}-\frac25=\frac{22}{1445}>r,\qquad
 \frac25-\frac{203}{578}=\frac{141}{2890}>2r.
\]
All other relevant margins are larger.  Consequently the full graph is
unchanged throughout the parameter family and
$B(w_i)=S_0\cup\{p_i\}$.  In particular, the forgotten feature $p_i$ does
not vary with the hidden feature $w_i$.

Define
\[
 C=aa^T+2\lambda I_d,\qquad c=1+2\lambda,\qquad
 \beta_0=\frac\lambda{2c}.
\]
Choose $\beta_i\in(\beta_0,2\beta_0)^q$ and set
\[
 y_i=(1+w_i^TC^{-1}w_i)\beta_i.
\]
Every non-candidate response is zero.  Since
$1+w_i^TC^{-1}w_i\le c/(2\lambda)$, each response coordinate is in
$(0,1/2)$.  This is a coordinatewise bound, not a dimension-independent
bound on $\|y_i\|_2$.

Initially only $a$ is selected and its head is zero.  No candidate can be
selected within budget $b$, so every such target is zero.  At budget $b+1$,
request $F_i=S_0\cup\{p_i\}$ selects exactly $\{a,w_i\}$.  Conversely any
request of size at most $b+1$ selecting a candidate must equal its $F_i$.
Its normal equations and Sherman--Morrison solution are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phase9-realizable-head}
 (C+w_iw_i^T)W_i=w_i y_i^T,
 \qquad W_i=C^{-1}w_i\beta_i^T.
\end{equation}
Thus the actual ridge output identifies the private parameters:
\[
 w_i=\frac{CW_i[:,1]}{\|CW_i[:,1]\|_2},\qquad
 \beta_{ij}=\|CW_i[:,j]\|_2.
\]
Positivity fixes the sign.  This argument uses realizable ridge heads,
without treating Gram and cross moments as independent free coordinates.

\subsection{Exact coordinate requirement}
\begin{theorem}[Realizable adjacent-budget dimension transition]
Under the preceding dimension/signature and access assumptions, restrict
the private features to the open cap charts below.  The minimum fixed
number of real coordinates in a continuous deterministic exact model-query
encoder is zero at horizon $b$, and exactly $m(d-1+q)$ at horizon $b+1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each center take a public orthonormal tangent frame $V_i$ and write
\[
 w_i(t_i)=\sqrt{1-\|t_i\|_2^2}\,w_i^0+V_i t_i,
 \qquad \|t_i\|_2<r/2.
\]
The displacement from $w_i^0$ is less than $r$, and
$t_i=V_i^Tw_i$.  Together with the open response intervals this gives an
open domain in $\mathbb R^M$, where $M=m(d-1+q)$.
If two parameter tuples produced the same encoded state, exact responses
to every $F_i$, using their identical public forgotten payloads, would be
identical.  The recovery formulas force all parameters to agree.  The
encoder is therefore injective.  A continuous injection from an open
subset of $\mathbb R^M$ into $\mathbb R^s$ with $s<M$ is impossible:
composition with coordinate inclusion into $\mathbb R^M$ contradicts
invariance of domain, since the image lies in a proper coordinate subspace.
Hence $s\ge M$.

For sufficiency store $(t_i,\beta_i)_{i=1}^m$, use
\eqref{eq:phase9-realizable-head} on $F_i$, and return zero on every other
allowed request.  This is a continuous encoder using $M$ coordinates.
At horizon $b$ always return zero.
\end{proof}

Any sequential exact service with a continuous initial encoder inherits
the lower bound, since each $F_i$ must be supportable as its first request.
The mathematical distinguishability argument does not require operational
rollback.  The upper bound remains a model-query summary rather than a
canonical state-repair theorem.  An arbitrary discontinuous encoder can
encode many reals into one; continuity of the decoder alone would not
justify this lower bound.

For fixed $b$, the high-budget eligible count is
$E=N=1+b+2m=\Theta(m)$, so the lower bound is
$\Theta(E(d+q))$.  This matches the feature/response order of the existing
joint-span representation in a worst-case coordinate sense.  It excludes
metadata, precision, temporary memory, and bit complexity and does not
show a physical-byte improvement over the compact eligible-payload baseline.

\subsection{Finite-bit obstruction with explicit approximation scale}
For unit $w,w'$ the exact identity
\[
 \|w\beta^T-w'\beta'^T\|_F^2
 =\|\beta-\beta'\|_2^2+(\beta^T\beta')\|w-w'\|_2^2
\]
and $\sigma_{\min}(C^{-1})=1/c$ imply
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phase9-separation}
 \|W-W'\|_F\ge\frac1c
 \sqrt{\|\beta-\beta'\|_2^2+\beta_0^2\|w-w'\|_2^2}.
\end{equation}
The same cap chart obeys
$\|w(t)-w(t')\|_2\ge\|t-t'\|_2$ by tangent projection.
For packing only, allow the closed radius-$r/2$ chart ball and the closed
intervals $[\beta_0,2\beta_0]$; they still obey the cap and response bounds,
with responses at most $1/2$.

Fix $0<\eta<r\beta_0/(16c)$.  A maximal
$4c\eta/\beta_0$-separated packing of the chart ball has at least
\[
 L_w\ge\left(\frac{r\beta_0}{8c\eta}\right)^{d-1}
\]
points: balls of the separation radius cover the original ball, and volume
comparison proves the count.  A coordinate grid of spacing $4c\eta$ in
$[\beta_0,2\beta_0]^q$ has
\[
 L_\beta=\left(\left\lfloor\frac{\beta_0}{4c\eta}\right\rfloor+1\right)^q
\]
points.  The product code has $L=L_wL_\beta$ distinct heads separated by at
least $4\eta$, by \eqref{eq:phase9-separation}.

\begin{theorem}[Realizable feature-scale finite-bit bound]
Suppose a summary with at most $B$ private bits answers every fixed input
in the finite product construction and every $F_i$ to Frobenius error at
most $\eta$ with probability at least $1-\delta$, where
$0\le\delta<1/2$.  Public randomness independent of the private data and
fresh repair randomness are allowed.  Then
\[
 B\ge m\bigl[(1-\delta)\log_2 L-h_2(\delta)\bigr]
   \ge m\bigl[(1-\delta)(d-1+q)-h_2(\delta)\bigr].
\]
The explicit packing expressions give the stronger logarithmic bound at
smaller error.  The admissible fixed error depends on $\lambda$ and $r$,
but not on $m,d,q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose independent uniform packing symbols $Z_1,\ldots,Z_m$.  Let $M_s$
denote the private state and $R$ the public randomness.  Nearest-head
decoding recovers $Z_i$ on every successful answer.  The optimal decoder
from $(M_s,R,i)$ is no worse than randomized repair.  M-ary Fano yields
\[
 H(Z_i\mid M_s,R)\le h_2(\delta)+\delta\log_2(L-1)
                      \le h_2(\delta)+\delta\log_2 L.
\]
Conditional subadditivity and independence now give
\[
 B\ge I(Z_1,\ldots,Z_m;M_s\mid R)
 \ge m\log_2L-\sum_i H(Z_i\mid M_s,R).
\]
There is no simultaneous-success assumption.  If correctness is required
only on average over the uniform probe index, apply Fano with the individual
error probabilities and use concavity of binary entropy to average them.
All forgotten probe payloads are fixed independently of the symbols.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The feature obstruction with fixed public binary labels}
The feature-dimension conclusion does not require variable or
feature-dependent labels.  In the same graph/cap family take one response
coordinate, give every candidate the public label one, and give every
anchor/blocker the public label zero.  These responses are in $\{0,1\}$;
this is a separate response class from the preceding at-most-$1/2$ family.

\begin{corollary}[Public binary labels]
Under the same feature and access assumptions the exact continuous
model-query state minimum is zero at horizon $b$ and $m(d-1)$ coordinates
at horizon $b+1$.  For $0<\eta<r\lambda/(8c^2)$ a finite packing gives
\[
 L_w\ge\left(\frac{r\lambda}{4c^2\eta}\right)^{d-1},\qquad
 B\ge m\bigl[(1-\delta)\log_2L_w-h_2(\delta)\bigr]
\]
under the preceding finite-bit randomized correctness contract.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The hard head is $W=C^{-1}w\beta(w)$, where
$\beta(w)=1/(1+w^TC^{-1}w)\ge2\lambda/c>0$.
Normalization of $CW$ recovers $w$.  Continuous injectivity and chart
storage prove the exact dimension statement.  The same rank-one identity
gives
\[
 \|W-W'\|_2\ge\frac{2\lambda}{c^2}\|w-w'\|_2.
\]
A chart packing with separation $2c^2\eta/\lambda$ has the displayed
cardinality and head separation at least $4\eta$; apply the same Fano proof.
\end{proof}

The finite-bit theorem is separate from the continuous-coordinate theorem.
It uses a separated real-valued input family, not a demonstrated packing in
the repository's fixed FP32 domain.  Quantization must preserve graph margins
and head separation before claiming such a transfer.  The error scale can
be numerically small; no practical large-tolerance or optimal lossy-codec
claim follows.  Nor do these theorems establish privacy erasure, a uniform
natural-corpus lower bound, or NLP empirical evidence.

\paragraph{Attribution and reproducible checks.}
This extends the earlier activation-frontier architecture.  The additional
content is independent private feature variation with fixed forgotten
payloads, full-sphere identifiable realizable ridge heads, and the resulting
feature/response memory dependence.  Sherman--Morrison, invariance of domain,
volumetric packing, and Fano/random-access lower bounds are classical.
For invariance of domain see Hatcher, \emph{Algebraic Topology}, Theorem~2B.3,
\url{https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf}; for Fano with side
information and packing see Scarlett and Cevher, arXiv:1901.00555v3,
Theorem~1 and Sections~2.3 and~5.2,
\url{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.00555}.
The retained standard-library checker
\texttt{empirical\_execution/phase9/check\_theory\_extension.py} verifies
bounded algebraic instances with exact rational arithmetic and source-bound
reports.  Those fixtures do not prove the topological or information-theoretic
statements by testing and are not synthetic empirical evidence.
