TMLR preparation checklist — 18 October 2026

Current paper scope
The paper establishes finite-horizon exact repair and finite-format private-state
bounds, and reports two failed model-calibration studies. It does not claim an
executed natural-text effect study, useful semantic deduplication, or a practical
speed/memory/utility advantage. Fully LLM pair annotation remains unchanged.

Prepared
- Official TMLR anonymous single-column style, embedded unchanged in the same
  standalone manuscript. Author-year references alphabetized; captions follow
  the template; technical appendices follow references.
- Executed findings foregrounded; unexecuted downstream protocol placed in an
  explicitly labeled appendix. Broader-impact and AI-assistance disclosures added.
- Both failed calibrations, the earlier unfavorable memory result, private-data
  replay limits, and original unmet human/empirical gates retained.
- Separate anonymous code/aggregate supplement, including exact source copies,
  explicit extract transformations, 102 calibration-cut records and 72 deidentified
  compute records. Clean extraction and aggregate arithmetic checked.
- Existing editor source compiled successfully. The native template's default
  "Under review" header is formatting only: no submission has occurred.

Cannot honestly be marked complete
- Rendered PDF visual inspection and final PDF export: native computer-use runtime
  failed before returning a screenshot. No alternate PDF was compiled. The PDF
  already generated in the open editor can be exported and checked when accessible.
- Full-text comparisons for Tadakamalla et al. and Dominici remain unavailable.
  The paper acknowledges their related scope and makes no result/novelty claim
  that depends on excluding them. This closes the wording action, not the access
  gap or a comprehensive novelty assessment.
- The original natural-text repair/utility/cost program remains unexecuted and
  blocked by failed calibration; TMLR targeting does not waive that gate.

Author-supplied information required before an actual submission
- Final legal author list and affiliations; every author meets TMLR contribution
  and responsibility criteria and consents. OpenReview profiles must be complete.
- Current annual submission quota and no conflicting concurrent archival
  submission or overlapping prior archival publication.
- Accurate funding, competing interests, conflicts and applicable human-subjects/
  IRB declarations. Zero new human pair annotation does not determine every IRB
  or personal-data declaration for a study of existing online records.
- Appropriate action-editor suggestions and license/rights attestations. TMLR
  submissions use CC BY 4.0; private corpus/model rights are separate. No raw
  corpus, model weights or pair observations are included in the supplement.

These are not invented or auto-approved by an AI assistant. AI systems are not
authors. Fully LLM annotation is separate from human author accountability.
No external submission, author contact, consent, or legal acceptance was made.
Do not upload the full public development/history archive as anonymous material.
Use only the reviewed anonymous manuscript PDF and the dedicated supplement ZIP.
