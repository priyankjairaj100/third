# Supervised real-data suite: audited recommendation

Design note, 3 October 2026. No datasets were downloaded and no redundancy, activation, or performance outcomes were measured. This is a fixed execution protocol, not empirical evidence.

## Recommendation

Use **Civil Comments** for a scalar human-label regression experiment and **Stack Exchange questions** for a genuinely different multi-label NLP task and contributor-group withdrawals. Keep Amazon Reviews 2023 as a preregistered optional replication after the corpus terms are resolved. Do not make Yelp or 20 Newsgroups a dependency of the paper. No generated documents, paraphrase augmentation, fabricated duplicate families, label swaps, or graph rewiring are required.

The important provenance distinction is explicit: Civil Comments offers article/publication identifiers but no commenter identifiers. Stack Exchange offers `OwnerUserId`, which identifies the account associated with a question, not exclusive ownership of every token after community editing. Neither metadata field establishes a legal authorization to demand erasure.

## 1. Civil Comments: main exact scalar ridge benchmark

**Verified primary source.** TensorFlow's maintained catalog describes the dataset, confirms that CC0 covers the underlying comment text, exposes `id`, `article_id`, `publication_id`, `parent_id`, `created_date`, `text`, and seven crowd-label proportions in version 1.2.4. It lists 1,804,874 training, 97,320 validation, and 97,320 test examples. It explicitly warns that existing splits ignored parent relations. Use the ordinary CivilComments configuration, not Covert (the latter has a documented quality warning).

Source: https://www.tensorflow.org/datasets/catalog/civil_comments (web ref turn102view0). Raw alternative and full metadata: https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/jigsaw_unintended_bias/blob/main/README.md (turn102view1). Google card identifies CC0 and original metadata; pin exact source revisions and checksums at acquisition, not mutable `main`.

**Task.** Predict the original continuous `toxicity` fraction from comment text. Exact primary learner is scalar ridge with average loss and fixed regularization. Primary task metric is mean squared error against the observed human-label fraction; report MAE and AUROC of the prespecified thresholded event `toxicity >= 0.5` as secondary metrics. Unlearning fidelity is measured against full retained-corpus retraining, not against an assumed objective truth of toxicity.

**Text.** Use only each comment's own text. Do not prepend `parent_text`, metadata, publication, labels, or identity tags. This preserves a record-local loss and avoids an undeclared dependence on deleted parent comments. Preserve punctuation, casing, negation, spelling, and duplicates; normalize only Unicode NFC and whitespace for embedding input. Keep separate exact-byte and normalized-text hashes. Exclude only empty/invalid text and invalid labels; log all exclusions. No minimum-length rule in the primary corpus: short repeated replies may be the natural phenomenon.

**Deletion units.** (i) individual comments; (ii) all in-scope comments sharing `(publication_id, article_id)`; (iii) an entire publication, as a coarse source stress test. Missing/sentinel article IDs are unique singleton groups, never one artificial mega-source. Publications are observed provenance groups, not commenter identities. Source requests must include all records of that source within the experimental raw training corpus, including records originally suppressed by curation.

**Corpus sizes.** Lock a 200,000-record target for full supervised experiments and a nested 50,000-record development version. Select complete article groups by a seeded uniform hash ordering until their cumulative size first reaches the target; retain the complete last group and report actual N. Do not sample individual records within chosen article groups, oversample duplicates, or balance labels. Use all eligible comments for a structural-only scale audit if hardware permits. This group-based sample estimates behavior in a sampled collection of complete article discussions, not a record-uniform sample of all comments; report that estimand.

**Evaluation.** Use only the official training partition as the pool for the new primary benchmark. Form article groups (merging exact-copy groups across articles), hash them to 70/15/15 train/development/test, and apply the 200,000-record complete-group sampling rule inside training. Use up to 50,000 development and 50,000 primary test records via the same complete-group sampling rule. This yields an article-disjoint primary holdout; it must also exclude remaining high-confidence near-copy overlap with raw training, using a development-locked detector. Keep the original official validation/test partitions untouched and use their test only as a secondary comparability view. Report both raw and cleaned official holdout performance and the amount removed; do not silently replace the test distribution. Preserve a fixed test set across every request and every algorithm. The original parent relation and article metadata make this audit stronger than a random sentence split.

**Label fidelity.** Similar or identical comments can legitimately have different crowd-label fractions. Never force labels to agree during deduplication. Quantify selected-suppressed label discrepancy, stratified by cosine bin and length, using labels only after curator configuration is locked. Human duplicate adjudicators see text pairs with provenance masked and do not see toxicity scores. Identity annotations are incomplete and describe mentions, not author demographics; any subgroup analysis must use that meaning and report denominators and missingness.

## 2. Stack Exchange: main source-withdrawal and multi-label benchmark

**Verified primary sources.** Stack Overflow's help page states versioned CC BY-SA terms by contribution date: https://stackoverflow.com/help/licensing (turn86search0). The official Meta schema documents `OwnerUserId`, `Title`, `Body`, `CreationDate`, `Tags`, `PostTypeId`, and `PostLinks`; missing owners correspond to deleted accounts: https://meta.stackexchange.com/a/2678/242800 (turn86search5). The current release catalog links company-issued April 2024 XML/7z archives and newer community mirrors: https://meta.stackexchange.com/questions/224873/all-stack-exchange-data-dump-releases (turn97view1). Current official download routes changed in 2024; do not assume an unauthenticated latest-dump URL works: https://meta.stackexchange.com/questions/401324/announcing-a-change-to-the-data-dump-process (turn99search3).

**Fixed release.** Use the corrected company-issued April 2024 release, taking only the non-Meta `english.stackexchange.com` and `askubuntu.com` archives, `Posts.xml` and `PostLinks.xml`. The catalog explicitly distinguishes an initial buggy April release; never use it. Record archive hash and exact source URL. These are two predefined domains in one platform replication, not two independent replications of platform diversity. Ask Ubuntu tests technical prose; English Language & Usage tests general language questions. If acquisition of the fixed release fails, stop this arm and report the blocker; do not silently substitute a small HF clustering dataset with stripped provenance.

**Records and labels.** A record is an original question (`PostTypeId=1`), title plus HTML-to-text body, from 2018-05-02 through 2023-12-31 inclusive. Date restriction simplifies contribution-license handling, but retain per-post attribution and snapshot metadata. Exclude community-owned questions from contributor-withdrawal experiments; keep a documented record-deletion-only sensitivity version. Select the 20 most frequent original tags in the training partition and predict their multi-hot vector by multi-output ridge. Every eligible question remains, including questions with none of these 20 tags; no label-conditioned record filtering. Tag labels are observed community annotations, not complete ground truth. Report micro/macro AUPRC and sample-averaged squared error; fix thresholds for F1 on development data only. Never feed tags, duplicate notices, votes, closure status, or owner IDs into the text classifier.

**Text handling.** Remove HTML navigation artifacts and dataset-provided closure notices but retain the question's own prose, code, and quoted text. Use one fixed parser whose configuration is part of the data manifest. Code may dominate similarity; report prose-only as a named robustness condition, never select between versions by desired activation size. Title and body are two fields of a single real record, not synthetic text generation. Do not concatenate answers from other people.

**Deletion units.** Individual question, contributor account `(site, OwnerUserId)`, and site-level as a deliberately coarse stress test only. Missing owners are singletons for record deletion and excluded from the contributor sampling frame. For source withdrawals, retain all eligible questions from the sampled contributor inside D. Contributor identity is an account proxy; edits mean it is not an authorship guarantee. Do not join identities across sites using display names.

**Size and sampling.** Use every eligible English question if there are at most 200,000. Otherwise select complete owner groups by hash until the 200,000 target is reached. Apply the same 200,000 target to Ask Ubuntu. Source-unattributed records can form singleton groups in record-only results. Report actual counts and size distributions. Domain/time filters are fixed before inspecting unlearning outcomes. Do not use duplicate links to enrich the training sample.

**Independent semantic evidence.** `PostLinks` duplicate relations are a valuable human/community signal to audit cosine edges and deletion-induced entrants. They are neither exhaustive nor error-free, and should not define the primary curation graph; otherwise the empirical conclusion becomes circular. Keep the link relation for split leakage control and an independent duplicate-quality evaluation. Stratify manual examples into link-supported, cosine-only, lexical-near-copy, and apparently distinct.

**Splits.** Build leakage components from exact normalized copies and original duplicate links; assign components by a fixed hash to 70/15/15 train/development/test. Keep each question intact. Account overlap between components is permitted in this primary topic task and must be reported; the unlearning corpus D is training only. Add a source-disjoint evaluation, with contributor groups hashed before splitting and cross-split duplicate-linked/copy evaluation examples excluded. Do not claim both source and duplicate independence if only one was enforced. If connected components are giant, report their mass and allocate any component above 5% of eligible records to training by a prespecified rule; do not repeatedly reseed for a favorable test set.

## Shared reviewer-facing safeguards

1. Deduplicate only after defining D and sealing evaluation sets. Raw duplicates stay in D; do not apply the proposed curation while constructing the raw training universe.
2. Choose similarity thresholds by independent semantic-duplicate judgments on development-only pairs, targeting a declared precision level (e.g. 95% with confidence interval), then report a fixed sensitivity grid. A threshold chosen to maximize activation, speedup, or label changes invalidates the natural-prevalence claim.
3. No label balancing or deletion requests targeted by test error. Primary requests are uniformly sampled units and empirical size-stratified source units. A high-activation graph-only adversarial workload is a separate stress test with its construction cost charged.
4. Source unit and corpus scope are part of every metric row. Distinguish account-, article-, and publication-group withdrawals; a random batch is not an author deletion.
5. Measure both ordinary and rare events. Report the fraction of requests causing any addition, number of new entrants, label/gradient novelty of entrants, and contribution of entrants to test and retained-objective differences. Exact textual duplicates with identical features/labels may change the set but not normalized learning; label-conflicting and nonidentical entrants require separate reporting.
6. Freeze encoder, embedding normalization, priority/tie rule, lambda, and task decoding before requests. Curation anchors or centroids fitted on D cannot quietly count as external state. Either use truly independent calibration data or name the fixed-anchor counterfactual and run full refitting separately.
7. Return task metrics and counterfactual fidelity together. Large prediction disagreement can coexist with unchanged accuracy; high task accuracy can coexist with incorrect unlearning.
8. Release code, record-ID manifests, split hashes, request traces, aggregate measurements, and licenses/attribution instructions. Publish raw examples only under their applicable terms. No synthesized text is needed for the main empirical story.

## Audit of initially suggested alternatives

| Corpus | Useful facts | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Reviews 2023 | Official site verifies review text/title, 1–5 rating, reviewer ID, parent product ID, timestamp, direct category downloads. | Excellent third replication **after corpus-use terms are resolved**. Main card currently lacks a dataset-license field; a repository's MIT code license is not sufficient evidence that third-party review text has been relicensed. Do not predeclare MIT. |
| Yelp Open Dataset | Real `user_id`, `business_id`, text and stars; explicit academic/noncommercial agreement. | Optional only. Official 2021 agreement includes publication review/approval and a 12-month term, with restrictions on raw redistribution. Capture actual acquisition terms; not an unrestricted open-data dependency. |
| 20 Newsgroups | Fast diagnostic; actual headers can recover sender/thread proxies; category labels. | Optional appendix after license/source review. `20news-bydate` already removed duplicates and some headers; sender strings are noisy identities. sklearn's BSD license is code, not a verified corpus license. Strip topic-revealing headers and quoted context from model input if used. |
| AG News | Natural syndicated-news repetition and four topic labels. | Record-deletion diagnostic only after rights review. Official HF card labels license unknown and standard release exposes only text/label. Parsing `Reuters` from text does not create verified source ownership. |
| CFPB complaints | Government-hosted narratives and product/company metadata. | No need for core suite: company is the subject of a complaint, not its author/source; domain adds sensitive-content and label-taxonomy complications. |

Amazon source: https://amazon-reviews-2023.github.io/ (turn76view0); official card https://huggingface.co/datasets/McAuley-Lab/Amazon-Reviews-2023/blob/main/README.md (turn76view1).

Yelp agreement source, not a third-party summary: https://s3-media0.fl.yelpcdn.com/assets/srv0/engineering_pages/dc1cabe7cb95/assets/vendor/Dataset_User_Agreement.pdf (turn99view0). Relevant sections are 3, 4, and 10. This note does not assert that an older linked agreement replaces the terms supplied at a new download.

AG News official-maintainer card: https://huggingface.co/datasets/fancyzhx/ag_news (turn93view3). 20 Newsgroups maintained loader/documentation: https://scikit-learn.org/stable/datasets/real_world.html and https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn/blob/main/sklearn/datasets/_twenty_newsgroups.py (turn72search0–2).

## Decision to lock

Primary supervised scalar: Civil Comments, article withdrawal plus record deletion. Primary contributor replication: Stack Exchange English and Ask Ubuntu questions, contributor plus record deletion. The source-aware rank theorem is tested on observed provenance groups with their actual semantic meaning. Amazon optional; Yelp and 20NG nonessential. All natural-event rates remain unmeasured until the audit runs; no dataset is promoted for showing a favorable result.
