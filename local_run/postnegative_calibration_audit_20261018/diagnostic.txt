V4 calibration forensic review — 18 October 2026

Conclusion
No confirmed implementation defect that explains or reverses the v4 negative
calibration was found in this bounded source-and-receipt review. The native
Codex preview runtime is a different process from the completed Slurm inference.
Fixing the preview does not make the 19/20 model-agreement gate pass.

What was checked
- Inspected the frozen v4 plan, sampler, both native prompt renderers, generation
  wrapper, exact schema/word guard, repaired independent auditor and statistic core.
- Replayed all 51 saved cut decisions from fixed private label/sample inputs.
  Their hashes and seals agree, and every terminal cut reproduces exactly.
- Traced the prior newline bug. Six raw responses were changed by text-mode
  newline conversion in the old auditor. The exact-byte replacement already
  repaired the audit without a new model call, altered label or changed threshold.
- Both family audits attest complete pair identities, prompt/token reconstruction,
  grammar replay and full denominators. Five Qwen calls had independently verified
  JSONDecodeError; all remaining 787 calls were valid.

New descriptive sensitivity checks (not new acceptance rules)
- Maximum existing joint positive rate: 2/5 = 0.40.
- Even favorable treatment of every unavailable-format output: 13/30 = 0.4333.
- Favorable unavailable-plus-uncertain treatment: 8/15 = 0.5333.
- Qwen alone reaches at most 1/2; Mistral alone at most 5/6 on the frozen grid.
  Neither single model reaches 19/20, and selecting one would change the protocol.
Thus JSON repairs or removing the intersection rule cannot establish the
original required 0.95 agreement for these recorded outputs.

Limitations
The decoder intentionally constrains schema, words, token eligibility and
prefix stability. This can change output from unconstrained native decoding.
Its implementation asserts ranked finite-token selection, but there is no
independent full-logit replay or matched unconstrained control. This is an open
engineering diagnostic, not proof of a decoding bug or of semantic correctness.
Raw v4 source texts/reasons were not available in the local review bundle and
were not reannotated. No GPU inference, remote access, held-out inspection,
repository mutation or human pair annotation occurred in this review.

Concrete continuation

   validation/guard/effect admission. Original human gates remain unmet.
2. When access is restored and reconciled, fetch existing raw output and guard

4. A substantive model/prompt/representation change needs a new disclosed
