PROMPT, LABELS AND FAILURE HANDLING
Use six categories: exact_copy, substantially_same_meaning,
overlapping_information, merely_related, unrelated, uncertain.
Only the first two are positive. Require a short decisive reason and distinction
types (entity, quantity, negation, time, assertion, constraints, none, uncertain).
Show only the two texts and fixed task definition. Hide similarity, source/method,
prior labels, true tags, desired outcome, deletion request and model performance.
Treat document contents as untrusted quoted data; the judge has no tools/retrieval.
Harness-generated provenance stays outside model-generated JSON.
Keep every model response separately. Two positive model answers define the
conservative model-panel positive label. Binary positive/nonpositive disagreement
and uncertainty are nonpositive for threshold selection and remain flagged.
Different positive categories remain panel-positive and are logged as six-way
disagreement. Define y=1 iff BOTH model calls are valid and positive; otherwise y=0. Unavailable,
refused, overflow and invalid items contribute zero to the positive numerator
and retain their full sampling weight in the denominator. This is not majority
human judgment, and models are not assumed statistically independent.
No output, invalid JSON, overflow or refusal remains a distinct unavailable state.
Allow one logged format-only retry. Never retry until a desired semantic answer.
Unavailable states cannot silently disappear from denominators; report missingness
and conservative lower/upper sensitivity bounds. There is no self-confidence cutoff.
A prespecified 10% SRS of pairs is also judged with document order reversed. Those
calls measure position instability; they are not extra raters or extra votes.
Do not let models see or debate one another's answers before primary labels lock.

MODEL-ASSISTED CALIBRATION — NEW V2 ROLE ONLY
First establish accepted source inputs and a fully replayed frozen CPU/E5 cache.
The engineering pilot does not satisfy this dependency. Rebuild the pair frame
for that exact representation; do not transfer centered/GPU thresholds to raw CPU.
Enumerate the source-disjoint calibration population. Use 20 equal-width cosine
bins and up to 30 SRS pairs/bin, retaining complete bin counts/inclusion weights.
Lock v2 panel labels, then select the least strict existing grid threshold
(0.50 through 0.99 by 0.01, plus 0.995) with weighted model-panel precision>=0.95
and nonzero support. This is agreement with the model-defined label function.
Independently sample up to 200 from ALL pairs above that locked threshold. Query
fresh isolated model calls; overlap with selection remains allowed and disclosed.
Use a separate v2 evaluator. Model validation passes only when its one-sided 95%
exact finite-population lower bound is>=0.90 under the conservative y definition.
Freeze pair orientation by a stable hash, per-pair seed from the plan and opaque
pair ID, batch size 1, hardware/runtime and deterministic inference settings.
Selection/validation use the same canonical orientation and fixed label function.
Preserve first-pass labels; query overlapping validation pairs in fresh isolated
calls and check category agreement against their sealed selection labels. If
replay changes a category, report model validation unavailable due to instability;
never replace labels, vote over repeats or keep retrying. Swapped-order calls
remain separate diagnostics. Bounds condition on this specified fixed/potential
model-label function and appropriate sampling, not model correctness or independent
votes. Never use human-only ingestion APIs for these outputs.
No passing threshold: retain a failed diagnostic result; do not turn 0.995 into
success or choose a different representation/prompt after the validation outcome.
A validation failure stops this v2 configuration; revisions require a new version
and fresh evaluation data. Existing exposed pilots remain development history.
For one full cell: <=600 selection + 200 validation pairs, two models => 1,600 base
calls; 10% swaps add <=160. The technical pilot and transport retries are separate
budget lines. Human 100 is not multiplied by the number of models.

YOUR HUMAN AUDIT — TARGET 100, FALLBACK 50
Target 100 total: 80 simple-random probability-audit pairs plus 20 challenge pairs.
If only 50 are available: 40 probability-audit pairs plus 10 challenge pairs.
Reserve all these reviews until the models, prompt, threshold, audit frame,
sampling seed and reporting rule are sealed. Choose 100 versus 50 before the first
human response, solely from availability. No outcome-dependent extension or early
switch. Retain all planned units; incomplete reviews remain missing/nonpositive
for the conservative primary bound rather than shrinking n. These are not prompt-
training labels.
The probability frame is ALL calibration pairs with score>the locked threshold,
including model disagreement, nonpositive and unavailable cases. It is not merely
the model-consensus-positive subset, existing 600/200 sampled packs, or convenient
short documents. Log any overlap with exposed model pilots/selection; obtain
the author's first blinded response without showing earlier model judgments.
Sample SRS without replacement from the complete frame. Census
if the frame is smaller; report the actual population, sample and shortfall.
Challenge pairs are chosen separately before human answers: model disagreements,
natural entity/number/negation/time differences, near-threshold and long inputs,
and native linked duplicates where available. Use remaining distinct units;
keep their counts separate from probability estimates. Do not invent documents.
Randomize display order, opaque pair IDs and left/right orientation; hide stratum,
model answers/explanations, similarity, true tags, algorithms and desired outcome.
Show complete original task text; record uncertainty/skips, not forced answers.
Save the author's original response before showing model labels or discussing it.
No replacement of skipped/uncomfortable/difficult pairs with easier ones.
This is one genuine reviewer. Record the author's evaluator role and the limits
of author involvement; do not invent three people, independence or consensus.
If no threshold passes, the primary above-threshold audit does not exist. A
separately labelled diagnostic audit may examine the fixed engineering pair frame;
it cannot produce a threshold-quality pass. Do not consume the review budget on
that fallback without clearly presenting its different purpose to the author.

ANALYSIS AND CLAIMS
Report each judge and the fixed panel: six-way confusion, binary positive agreement,
disagreement/abstention, unavailable/overflow rate, order-instability, and cost.
On the probability audit, report the single reviewer's positive fraction among
ALL above-threshold pairs with an exact one-sided 95% finite-population lower bound.
Uncertain/skipped/missing is nonpositive for this conservative bound; also give
counts and a sensitivity range. Challenge results remain descriptive and separate.
A bound>=0.90 supports only the named single-reviewer/finite-frame claim for this
v2 curator, conditional on this author's fixed/potential protocol outcomes and
the sampling design. It does not quantify inter-rater or test-retest reliability,
establish three-rater agreement, or establish latent semantic truth.
For scale only, the large-population binomial approximation with zero nonpositive
reviews gives lower bounds 0.9632 for 80 and 0.9278 for 40. These are planning examples,
not observed results. Actual finite population size and observed ratings decide.
Do not estimate global duplicate recall from an above-threshold precision sample.
Do not fit bias corrections or choose the best judge on the same final audit and
then report the result as untouched validation. Any such change needs fresh audit.
Preserve the October 9 analysis lock for the original study. New v2 estimands and
any additional comparisons/multiplicity handling need their own pre-outcome lock.

BETTER OR COMPLEMENTARY ALTERNATIVES
A. Best anchor for the paper: exact comparisons to full recomputation plus original
   task labels, especially AskUbuntu top 20-tag prediction. These test repair and
   task utility without new semantic annotation. They do not prove human novelty.
B. Native AskUbuntu duplicate links: useful external positive anchors/challenge
   cases. Missing links are unlabelled, not verified negatives. Do not leak links
   used by preparation guards into a claimed untouched test, train a judge on
   its own evaluation anchors, or use WCEP shared event as duplicate ground truth.
C. Larger cross-family judges may improve annotation but require actual auditing;
   repeated calls to one model and multi-model consensus share possible biases.
D. Active learning is useful if the objective is improving a judge: prioritize
   disagreement examples for training. It is inferior to SRS for estimating
   prevalence/precision unless valid inclusion probabilities/weights are retained.
E. Prediction-powered inference can combine abundant model predictions with real,
   representative human labels. It is a possible later estimator, not a substitute
   for labels, sample design, or an underpowered 50–100 budget split across domains.
F. External crowdworkers/experts would be required to satisfy the original
   independent-three-rater design. They are unavailable now; no one is contacted.
