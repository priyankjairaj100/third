PRIMARY REFERENCES AND HOW THEY INFORM THIS PLAN
Read 11 October 2026. Literature findings are not validation on this project.

Gilardi, Alizadeh, Kubli (PNAS 2023), ChatGPT outperforms crowd workers for text-annotation tasks.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37463210/
Supports feasibility of LLM annotation on studied text-classification tasks;
does not establish semantic-equivalence accuracy on AskUbuntu or News.

Verga et al. (2024), Replacing Judges with Juries.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18796
Their diverse model panel improved agreement on studied generation-evaluation
benchmarks. Motivates comparing a panel with individual judges; not a guarantee.

Kim et al. (ICML 2025), Correlated Errors in Large Language Models.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/kim25e.html
Documents shared errors even across architecture/provider families. Model votes
cannot be counted as independent humans or used to invent an effective sample size.

Angelopoulos et al. (2023), Prediction-Powered Inference.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.09633
Uses real labeled data to correct model prediction bias. Could support a future
single-author-reference estimate under an appropriate design; does not remove
the need for labels or make a small nonrepresentative audit automatically sufficient.

Abbas et al. (2023), SemDeDup.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09540
Shows downstream performance/efficiency as substantive deduplication outcomes.
Supports our recommendation to emphasize original-label utility and complete cost.

TensorFlow Datasets, Civil Comments.
https://www.tensorflow.org/datasets/catalog/civil_comments
Documents existing crowd-label fractions. These support toxicity-related task
evaluation if authentic source inputs become available; they are not pairwise gold.

Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct official model card.
https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct
Documents model identity and default 32768-token configuration; longer contexts
require separate choices. Cached local revision observed in this planning turn.

Mistral-NeMo-Instruct official model card and model API.
https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Mistral-Nemo-Instruct-2407
https://huggingface.co/api/models/mistralai/Mistral-Nemo-Instruct-2407
Observed revision 04d8a90549d23fc6bd7f642064003592df51e9b3, ungated, 12,247,782,400
BF16 parameters. Apache 2 documentation and architecture motivate a feasible
cross-family candidate, not a claim of superior annotation quality.

Qwen3-32B official model card.
https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-32B
A larger alternative with explicit thinking/non-thinking modes. Approximate
BF16 tensor memory is a parameter-count estimate, not a measured serving footprint.
Its model-specific decoding settings would require a new frozen configuration.
