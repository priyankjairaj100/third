14 October 2026. Model-defined evidence only. Not activated; no new calls or samples.

DECISION AND SCOPE
The user explicitly requires no human annotation. This branch has zero planned
human reviews, zero human responses, and no human collection dependency. The
unexecuted v2 50–100-author-review plan is historical. The original independent
three-human gate remains unmet; it is not an activation gate for this new LLM-only
branch. No model response may be called human-completed or human-equivalent.
Original locks, frozen phases, v2 files, and all failed/negative evidence remain
unchanged. This changes the evidential target, not the historical facts.

Start with AskUbuntu/E5. Use the frozen CPU FP32 E5 representation and original
source-disjoint preparation. Known preparation counts are 140,250 rows, of which
20,996 are calibration rows (220,405,510 unordered pairs). These counts are checks
to reproduce, not admission evidence by declaration. All unordered pairs within
the admitted calibration partition are eligible, including same-source pairs.
Source-disjoint means between train/calibration/test partitions, not exclusion of
same-source calibration pairs. A completed sharded cache
assembly and a bounded scorer benchmark exist; a separate scientific source/cache
adapter and complete pair-frame census still must pass. Do not promote a metadata
review or the old GPU/centered engineering pack to that gate. News/E5 can follow
only under its own source/frame activation. Do not add an MPNet arm here.

Actual original dataset targets, exact numerical repair, and measured total cost
remain separate useful outcomes. Original human relevance/admission/context
endpoints remain unmeasured. They are not silently replaced by model judgments.
A future model-assessed interpretation endpoint needs its own prospectively locked
context, sampling design and estimand; this package does not define that endpoint.

LABEL FUNCTION AND FULL DENOMINATOR
The six categories remain exact_copy, substantially_same_meaning,
overlapping_information, merely_related, unrelated, uncertain. Only the first
two are positive. y=1 only if BOTH model responses are valid and positive; all
other pairs have y=0 for the conservative primary calculation. Different positive
categories remain panel-positive, while their six-way disagreement is recorded.
Disagreement, uncertainty, missing output, refusal, invalid schema, context
overflow and runtime failure retain their distinct statuses and their entire
sampling weight in the denominator. Report missingness and sensitivity bounds
separately. Neither confidence scores nor assumed independent model errors are
used. No unavailable, inconvenient, long or previously exposed pair is filtered
from the population or silently replaced.

SELECTION AND INDEPENDENT VALIDATION
Use the complete source-disjoint calibration pair population and frozen FP32-to-
FP64 coordinate-ordered normalization/dot product. Keep raw scores unclipped;
bin assignment alone saturates at the first/last bin. Use strict score > tau.
Selection has 20 equal-width bins over [-1,1] with nearest-FP64 decimal edges,
left closed/right open except the final closed endpoint. Take reservoir SRS up
to 30 per bin, census smaller bins, and retain exact bin populations/inclusion
probabilities. At most 600 selection pairs. The estimator is the ratio of
Horvitz-Thompson weighted positives to weighted sampled above-threshold pairs.
Select the least strict grid threshold 0.50,0.51,...,0.99,0.995 with ratio >=0.95
and nonzero support. Preserve the entire grid ledger. No passing threshold means
0.995 is a failed diagnostic value, never a successful threshold.

After locking selection outputs and the threshold, independently sample up to
200 from ALL score>tau calibration pairs. Census if N<200; empty population or
empty sample has undefined precision and fails. Selection/pilot overlap is allowed
and reported; exclusion of overlap would change the design. Query fresh isolated
canonical calls for BOTH models on every validation pair. Do not reuse selection
answers. The one-sided 95% exact finite-population lower proportion must be >=0.90.
The frozen numerical bound is retained with alpha=1/20 and threshold=9/10; its
label target becomes the prospective conservative two-model function. This does
not establish semantic precision, human agreement, latent truth or global recall.
No threshold, representation, judge or prompt is changed after validation to pass.
A failed configuration remains failed; revisions need a new version and properly
separated evaluation with earlier exposure disclosed.

Keep canonical orientation and each model's seed identical across selection and
validation. For every overlap, compare each model's original status and valid
six-way category. Any changed category OR changed status/availability makes model
validation unavailable due to instability even if its numeric lower bound passes.
Do not vote over repeats, overwrite labels, retry for agreement, or drop the pair.
A reason-only wording change is not category instability. Zero overlap must be
reported as zero replay evidence. Observed agreement never proves full-frame
stability; finite-population coverage conditions on the fixed/potential label
function and the stated uniform-draw sampling model.

A separate 10% SRS of each stage's sampled pairs (ceil(n/10)) receives swapped-order
calls from both models. These are diagnostics, never extra votes or replacements.
Use a separately committed swap seed for each stage. Maximum one-cell budget:
600+200 canonical pairs times two models =1,600 calls; swaps add at most160;
maximum1,760 calls, zero retries, zero human annotation. Pilot calls are historical
and not included in this prospective budget. Record all failure and process costs.

V3 IDENTITIES, RANDOMNESS AND ORDER
The exact formulas and domains are specified in PLAN.json and protocol.py.
Canonical serialization is UTF-8 JSON, ensure_ascii=False, sorted keys, compact
separators, no NaN and no text/ID Unicode normalization. Hash structured arrays,
not ambiguous delimiter concatenation. Domain prefixes are distinct for sampling,
natural pairs, opaque IDs, orientation, model seeds and call identity.
Before any selection observation, generate independent256-bit OS seeds for
selection, validation, selection swaps and validation swaps, and seal all four in
the new activation lock. No seed search/regeneration based on scores or labels.
No random seed or sample is created by this planning package. Selection uses a
separate domain-derived Python random.Random stream per bin; validation/swap
streams are separate. As in phase3, SRS refers to the uniform-draw reservoir
algorithmic model, not a proof of cryptographic or exact finite-seed randomness.

Natural-pair identity binds the full frame and UTF-8-byte-sorted native IDs;
opaque identity also binds this PLAN.json's raw SHA. Orientation uses its own
domain and those stable identities. Each model seed binds its exact revision and
opaque pair; it excludes stage/orientation so canonical fresh calls and diagnostic
swaps use the same seed. Call IDs additionally bind activation, stage, orientation
and attempt0, so separate observations cannot collide. Freeze the scorer's stream
order/block size before sampling; serialize samples in source row-index order.
Within each model/stage, schedule longest full prompt first, with frozen sample
index ties. No outcome-dependent scheduling or statelessness assumptions by fiat.
